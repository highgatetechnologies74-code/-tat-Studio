Conceptual case study

The neighbourhood café that was selling coffee

The challenge
Steady footfall, but only regulars knew what made the place different.
The thinking
The coffee was good, not unique. The room, the sourcing and the people were. The message was aimed at the wrong asset.
The approach
Reposition around the café as a local meeting place, rewrite the core message and commit to two channels.
The outcome
Added when real evidence exists.

Conceptual case study

The design studio that attracted the cheapest quote

The challenge
A strong portfolio, but enquiries came from clients shopping on price.
The thinking
The work signalled craft while the messaging signalled availability. Customers read the second.
The approach
Define the ideal client, rewrite the proposition around outcomes and tighten how enquiries are handled.
The outcome
Added when real evidence exists.
